Two people with the same income walked into the same bank last year and walked out with different mortgage rates. Not slightly different. Nearly two full percentage points apart.

Here's the part nobody explains: the gap had almost nothing to do with how much they earned. According to the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau's own rate spread data, the single biggest driver was a three digit number neither of them fully understood.

That gap costs the second borrower about forty thousand dollars over the life of the loan. Same house. Same salary. Same bank.
